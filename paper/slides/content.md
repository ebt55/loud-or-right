# Slide deck content: "Loud or Right?" (16:9, 9 slides, low text)

Every number comes from `paper/report.md`; do not change any. Speaker notes are optional and short.

Built deck: `loud-or-right-slides.pptx` and `.pdf` (Space Grotesk + Inter, the main deck), generated by `src/build_deck.js` (pptxgenjs, 13.333 × 7.5 in), exported and checked by `src/export_and_check.py`. All slide text is at least 18 pt in the Segoe UI build; the main deck uses the same sizes × 0.93.

**Palette and type**
- Colours:
  - dark ink #14213D;
  - paper #FBFBF8;
  - mist #EEF1F6 (alternate background);
  - open-weight blue #2F6FD1;
  - closed orange #C8561F;
  - recency green #2E8B57;
  - soft text #4A5568.
- Headings: Space Grotesk, or a clean geometric sans if unavailable. Body: Inter, or Segoe UI or Arial.
  - As built: headings Space Grotesk Bold, body Inter (main deck); an alternative build uses Segoe UI Semibold and Segoe UI. ✓ and ✗ use Segoe UI Symbol in both.
- At most about 25 words of body text per slide (slide 9's references and credits excepted). Use big numbers, simple diagrams and the paper's figures.
- Background rhythm: dark (ink) for slides 1, 5 and 8; paper or mist for the rest. A practice-log card (voice tags plus ✓/✗) recurs on slides 1, 3 and 5 as the visual motif.

## 7. The cost of faithful reading
- Title: "Faithful is not the same as safe"
- **Bar visual,** titled "Correct answers abandoned, favourable log":

  - "Sonnet, brief reasoning: 8 of 160" (short grey bar);
  - "Sol: at most 2 of 40 per cell" (tiny dark-ink bar; blue is reserved for open-weight models).

## Font variants
