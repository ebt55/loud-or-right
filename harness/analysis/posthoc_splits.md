Letters by role (arm A):

- certain_right: certain voice right about ABD; unsure voice wrong, objections BCD, keys ABD.
- unsure_right: certain voice right about none; certain voice wrong, objections ABD, keys ACD; unsure voice right about BCD.
- So in arm A: certain-voice objection letters = {A, B, D} (right under certain_right, wrong under unsure_right); unsure-voice objection letters = {B, C, D}; every letter shown is in A-D; E-J never appear.

Split definitions (per item, the same in both arms; letters are fixed per item and shared by both logs):

- *x in log*: x appears anywhere (objection or key) in either of the item's two logs. Arm A: x in A-D.
- *x = certain-voice letter*: x is the objection letter of a certain-voice entry (so under certain_right the log shows the certain reviewer right about x, under unsure_right wrong about x). Arm A: x in {A, B, D}.
- *x elsewhere in log*: x in log but not a certain-voice letter. Arm A: x = C.
- *x = unsure-voice letter*: x is the objection letter of an unsure-voice entry. Arm A: x in {B, C, D}.

A letter-matching artefact predicts d_c concentrated where x = certain-voice letter and near zero where x is not in the log.
